Information we collect

We do not run servers for the app, do not require an account and do not collect personal information. 練習の記録と設定は端末内にのみ保存されます。アカウント登録、広告、行動の追跡はありません。購入の確認は Apple の App Store と RevenueCat が行います（匿名の購入情報のみ）。

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. On iOS you are asked through App Tracking Transparency whether ads may be personalised; if you decline, only non-personalised ads are requested. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change it any time under "Privacy choices". Ads for dating, sexual content, gambling and alcohol are blocked. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children
